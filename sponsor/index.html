Why sponsor Lovelace

Every part of the day that costs money — travel and accommodation for poster presenters, cash prizes, lunch, coffee, and the evening social — is funded by sponsorship and donations rather than a ticket price, because the event is free for every student who attends. Sponsoring Lovelace puts your organisation in front of some of the best undergraduate and MSc computing students in the UK, at exactly the stage they're starting to think about placements, graduate schemes and first jobs.

Ways to support the event

  • Sponsor a prize or a part of the day — headline sponsorship, a year-group poster prize, lunch, coffee, or the evening social have historically each been sponsored individually.
  • University partnership & supporter status — if you're a university rather than a company, you can pledge to cover your own students' travel, or donate so the cost is covered centrally.
  • Bring a stand — sponsors and recruiters are welcome to have a table at the event alongside the poster session.
  • Donate — a general donation to keep the event financially sustainable, no sponsorship package required.
