What to expect on the day

Lovelace is a one-day, in-person event built around three things: talks from women in computing across academia and industry, an open poster contest where students present and discuss their own work, and plenty of unstructured time to network with students from universities across the UK. Most years also include a careers panel and a social in the evening before or after the main day.

Past events

Lovelace has run every year since 2008, moving to a different UK university each time — Leeds, Cardiff, Birmingham, Bath, Nottingham, Reading, Edinburgh, Sheffield, Aberystwyth, Manchester, Salford and Glasgow among the venues so far. Lovelace 2026 was hosted by the University of Bath.
