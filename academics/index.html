For lecturers & departments

Support Lovelace from your department

Encourage your students to submit a poster, put your department forward to host a future Colloquium, or become a University partner pledging to cover your own students' travel.

Encourage your students to take part

The single biggest thing a department can do is simply tell its women and non-binary students that Lovelace exists — many students only hear about the poster contest through a lecturer forwarding the call for abstracts. Consider mentioning it in a lecture, on a course noticeboard, or in a departmental newsletter when the call opens each autumn.

University partnerships & supporter status

Because the audience is entirely undergraduate and MSc students, covering travel is central to how Lovelace runs — students are put up in a small number of hotels together for safety and social reasons, so accommodation is usually booked and paid for centrally rather than left to individual students.

Institutions can support this in two ways: pledge to cover the travel (and where relevant, accommodation) of their own students who take part, or donate to the event so BCSWomen can cover those costs centrally on their behalf. Recent university partners and supporters have included Aberystwyth, Bath, University of Birmingham, London South Bank University, Sheffield, Sheffield Hallam, Southampton, University of the West of England and Warwick.

Host a future Colloquium

Lovelace moves to a different UK university roughly every year — past hosts include Leeds, Cardiff, Birmingham, Bath, Nottingham, Reading, Edinburgh, Sheffield, Aberystwyth, Manchester, Salford and Glasgow. If your department has the space for a poster hall, a lecture theatre for talks, and would like to put itself forward as a future host, get in touch with the leadership team.

Speak, judge or volunteer

Each Colloquium runs on a mix of formal talks and informal advice — keynote speakers, breakout sessions, careers panels, and poster judges drawn from academia and industry. If you'd like to speak, judge posters, or help run the day, the leadership team would like to hear from you.
